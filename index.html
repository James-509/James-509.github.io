Hanzi Exploration

2026

Used convolutional neural nets to predict pronunciations of Chinese characters from their image. As expected, traditional characters proved more fruitful for prediction, since they contain more etymological information. Augmenting the data with slight rotations and scaling also improved performance. Interestingly, there was a statistically significant correlation between correct pinyin prediction (without tone) and tone prediction, but only among traditional characters — again suggesting traditional characters carry more information about pronunciation.
